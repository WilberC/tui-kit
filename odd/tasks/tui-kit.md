# tui-kit

Public Go module (github.com/WilberC/tui-kit) holding the shared visual system of
herdr-suite and herdr-utils, extracted from their tested code, plus the design guide.

## Specs

- S1: "Podemos armar alguna libreria o algo similar con estilos o parametros que nos sirva para armar nuestras TUI? seria una libreria privada para seguir la linea visual y buenas practicas de diseño en TUI" — the owner then chose: a Go module in its own repo, public (so public release builds and `go install` fetch it without credentials), built after the redesign was committed by extracting the already-tested code.
- S2: Packages: `theme/` (Tokyo Night roles), `frame/` (frame, rules, footer), `chip/` (chips and hint bar), `list/` (rows, tree, selection), `text/` (fit, clip, ellipsis), and `DESIGN.md` (the visual guide).
- S3: The look stays exactly what the owner approved on 2026-11-13: tinted selection (bg #283457, accent ▌ edge, every element keeps its role), padded chip slabs, one blank row between groups, joined rules, 80 to 180 columns without wrapping.
- S4: herdr-suite and herdr-utils import the module with `go get` and drop their private copies; their screens render the same as before (byte-identical where the code moved unchanged).
- S5: Repository name: "tui-kit esta bien".
- S6: Hints that do not fit must stay reachable: "eso hay que fixearlo si es que no hay espacio debe de haber un scroll que deje mover o que indique que hay mas y poder verlos no puede simplemente no aparecer". The hint bar pages: when the hints do not fit, the bar shows the page that fits plus a final chip naming the paging key and the position (for example ` tab ` `more 1/2`); the key moves to the next page and wraps around. When everything fits, no paging chip is shown and the key does nothing. Every product TUI wires the key.

- S7: Size and anchoring: "veo mucho espacio en la parte de abajo, podemos hacer cosas especificas como tamaño maximo y minimo para evitar esos espacios vacios tan grandes, de todas formas el footer debe estar siempre pegado abajo y no dejar un espacio tan grande y en la imagen por ejemplo el cuerpo que es donde estan los hotkeys esta bien en el padding de top, pero falta el bottom padding que tambien debe tener siempre es importante para que las secciones sean distinguibles facilmente". The frame's height is clamped between a minimum and a maximum and never leaves the pane empty below it: inside the frame the footer is always the last rows (pinned to the bottom edge), the body takes the space between header and footer, and the body always has one blank row of padding at the top and one at the bottom. Products size their herdr popups in the manifest so the clamped frame fills them.
- S8: Mouse support, approved: "1) Si, me parece genial" for the proposal: the kit records where it drew each clickable element (rows, chips, the paging chip) and answers which element a click at a cell hit; each product decides what a click does. Click selects; double click or enter runs; a destructive action never fires on a single click; the wheel scrolls the body; hover reuses the selection tint, fainter; only chips and rows are clickable, never loose text; everything the mouse does is also reachable by keyboard.
- S9: Text selection, approved with S8: a TUI that captures the mouse does not reimplement text selection (gentle-shell does, because copying is central to a chat transcript); when a product needs to copy something specific, it offers one key that writes it with OSC 52. Owner: "no veo porque copiar algo que si deba de dar click en la TUI, pero es mejor tenerlo en cuenta con el ejemplo de gentle-shell".
- S10: The four follow-ups the product migrations asked for: HintBar resets itself when its hint set changes, a per-hint chip style (the gold y of a confirmation), a join that drops the right side first, and a Surface helper that frames body rows.
- S11: A committed visual gallery as the design reference, instead of Figma: "tienes razon - hay que agregar la gallery no esta mal y nos ayuda un montón a tener guia visual". docs/gallery holds PNGs generated from the real code by one command; DESIGN.md shows them next to each rule.
- S12: The follow-ups the products asked for in L14, shipped as v0.4.0 and adopted by both products: "has las mejoras de tui-kit y dejemos todo listo y aprobado en which, hs y tui-kit". (a) a zone-aware row helper for rows without a chip column; (b) a row with a trailing, right-aligned column for tables; (c) an accent chip role in list.Roles; (d) JoinDropRight keeps a minimum gap so products stop hand-padding the right side; (e) Surface can answer BodyRows from row counts, so a header that names the body's page no longer needs a placeholder; (f) DESIGN.md says a fact glyph (the default marker) may stay on a hovered row, that only a cursor mark may not, and that the hint bar resets when it draws.
- S13: Move the two remaining hand-placed row pieces into the kit (v0.4.1): "muevelo a tui-kit para que quede bien". (a) a row that hangs under a parent's guide (the switcher's nested workspace rows) draws and records its own zone, so herdr-suite stops adding ChildZone by hand; (b) a trailing column whose bytes match a hand-aligned right column (one background span), so the space picker's age column can move to Entry.Trailing without changing its bytes. Both products adopt it with byte-identical captures.
